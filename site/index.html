04 / Routes to MarketFever × Carahsoft · U.S. public sector · October 27

Make the purchase
possible.

Fever named Carahsoft its Master Government Aggregator for the U.S. public sector. The ticketing, visitor-management and audience-engagement platform gains a route through resellers and purchasing vehicles including SEWP V, NASPO ValuePoint and OMNIA Partners.

Our reading. A channel partner can contribute the procedure that lets a willing customer actually buy. Public bodies may need an eligible supplier, an established contract route and a defensible approval process as much as another product demonstration.

Ask your sales teamIs the customer unconvinced — or unable to complete the purchase through an acceptable route?

Routes to Market / Deal termsTempraMed × SPORTORO · Southeast Europe · Announced October 28

Exclusivity
has to be earned.

TempraMed announced a 36-month distribution agreement covering Croatia, Serbia, and Bosnia and Herzegovina. The agreement itself is dated October 19. SPORTORO is responsible for local approvals and market development; exclusivity depends on performance and other conditions, including annual minimum purchase targets.

The release includes an illustrative value of about US$550,000 over the initial term, assuming minimum targets at current prices and an all-VIVICap product mix. It explicitly says this is not a firm order, a guarantee or revenue guidance. Actual purchases could be materially lower.

Our reading. Territory, purchase obligations and the work of creating demand belong in the same negotiation. Rights without an execution plan are difficult to value.

Read the condition, not just the territoryWho funds market entry, what preserves exclusivity and what evidence shows that the distributor can deliver?
TempraMed company-issued release ↗

The release describes first-order payment terms, not evidence that the order has been fulfilled. Detailed annual unit schedules and margins are not disclosed.

05 / Deal EconomicsFortera × Meta · United States · October 29

Demand can help
a factory get built.

Fortera announced a multiyear agreement with Meta for environmental attribute certificates associated with approximately 200,000 tons of low-carbon cement. In this book-and-claim model, the environmental attribute is accounted for separately from the physical material.

Our reading. A committed buyer can matter before the next plant produces its first shipment. Visibility of future demand can strengthen a financing case. That is different from saying that a plant has secured its financing or delivered its promised environmental result.

Follow the object of the purchaseAre you buying the product, an attribute associated with it, future capacity — or a combination with separate obligations?
Fortera explanation and announcement ↗

Price, exact term, payment schedule and completed plant financing are not verified here. No realized emissions benefit is inferred from the announcement.

06 / OperationsDP World × Alce Nero · Italy · October 27

One anchor customer.
Room for more.

DP World launched its first Italian fast-moving consumer goods contract-logistics operation near Bologna, with Alce Nero as anchor customer. The multi-customer facility combines warehousing, transport and e-commerce fulfillment.

The site has 22,000 m² of space and approximately 30,000 pallet positions, operating at 18–20°C. This is temperature-controlled ambient storage; its specification should not be confused with a chilled-food warehouse.

Our reading. One customer’s recurring need can support a capability offered to several others. The practical questions are how much capacity is committed, how peaks overlap and who bears the cost of unused space.

DP World launch announcement ↗

The facility has launched. Occupancy, tariffs and measured cost or speed improvements are not disclosed.

Operations / Buyer’s DeskBlue Bird × LightSource · United States · Existing case study

Procurement
needs a memory.

Blue Bird’s sourcing history had been scattered across email and spreadsheets. A LightSource customer case describes standard bid templates and a shared record of supplier offers and decisions.

The case reports requests for quotation per buyer rising from 34.9 to 48.3 — about 38% — while the average cycle fell from 16 to 14 days. These are activity and cycle-time measures. They do not, by themselves, establish lower purchase prices.

For the supplierMake your offer easy to compare and retain: consistent units, a clear specification, price validity and the full document set.

The person in the process / Mandy Miller

Could someone reconstruct the decision?

Blue Bird’s purchasing manager for compliance systems and analytics describes the importance of controls and an accessible record for audit. This is drawn from the published customer case, not an interview conducted by B(ornot)2B.

07 / Reality CheckGigaHubs · United Kingdom · From the archive

The risk nobody
could control.

A Midlands procurement for connecting public buildings reached preferred-bidder stage. But the funding arrangement involved clawback if connected sites did not subscribe to broadband service. Nottinghamshire County Council was concerned about liability for organizations whose decisions it could not control.

The commissioned process review says the parties did not resolve the issue: the supplier was not appointed and the procurement closed without a contract.

Our reading. A risk allocation can stop a purchase even after the technology and supplier have been selected. Check whether the party carrying an obligation can influence the event on which it depends.

BDUK process review ↗

Published June 26, 2026; deliberately outside this issue’s news window. Belmana and GC Insight used documents, management data and interviews conducted September 2023–July 2024. This is a specific procurement case, not a claim that the whole program failed.

toward two more.

  1. 2024 / Commercial agreementThe move beyond a pilot.
  2. 2026 / Reported activityMore than 100,000 tote moves at the first site.
  3. 2027 / Planned expansionTwo additional sites; deployment has yet to begin.
What to check nextActual go-live dates, throughput including downtime and the cost per completed operation. A cumulative activity count is not a payback calculation.

Reality Check / The NumberWesco · United States · Results published October 29
16.6%Wesco Q3 sales growth
year on year

Take the growth
apart.

Wesco reported quarterly net sales of US$7,226.4 million. Data-center sales were approximately US$1.8 billion, up about 55%; sales outside data centers grew 7%. Total company backlog rose about 80% year on year.

Watch the rounding
The release headline uses US$7.2 billion and 17%; the results table provides the more precise figures above.
Look below the total
Different businesses can contribute very different growth rates. One aggregate number can hide that mix.
Keep the boundary
These are company-reported, unaudited quarterly results. Backlog is not recognized revenue, and one distributor is not the whole market.
