and delivery

This week’s stories share a question: what does a company buy to make its next promise deliverable? One acquires engine manufacturing rights. Others secure future feedstock, expand a chain’s product range or find cash during a payment term. Each agreement creates work that still has to become an outcome.

We therefore look beyond the agreement headline. Who prepares the factory and the stock? Who finances the wait? What has the customer actually committed to accept? The long reads connect specific disclosures to these questions. The issue ends with actions for a Monday meeting.

09 / Beyond the announcement

Beyond the announcement

These answers could change our assessment. They are where the next useful development should be found.

Who confirms independence?

In the Firefly–AST transfer, the revealing evidence will be a criterion showing when the receiving team can manufacture and resolve defects without continuing reliance on the original developer.

When does expansion become repeat business?

For Karat, replenishment and recurring demand after the initial shipment wave would be stronger evidence of an established account. The first shipments and additional agreements show only part of that story.

Who can afford a delay?

In supplier finance and new feedstock chains, waiting has a cash cost. We need evidence of available liquidity, confirmation times and the ability to revise commitments when a schedule slips.

10 / The Monday Move

The Monday Move

Turn the reading into one working meeting. Take an active deal and compare its commercial calendar with the operating calendar needed to deliver it.

  1. For the commercial leadName the buyer’s next observable action: approve an item, place an order, accept a shipment or replenish. Give each action a date and an owner.
  2. For the finance teamMark the days from the first paid cost to cash collection. Show the acceleration fee separately from the amount still exposed to a dispute.
  3. For the operations leadTest one backup: documentation, compatibility, people and available capacity. Record which condition authorises a switch.
