ABOUT THE MAGAZINE

Every Deal Has an Operating Reality

We examine sales, procurement, commercial terms, distribution, manufacturing, logistics, and payments. At the centre are real companies, documents, and the people who turn an agreement into execution.

Each issue includes full analyses on individual pages, the week’s briefs, working tools, and a book. Announcements are kept distinct from achieved results, and undisclosed terms remain unknown.
