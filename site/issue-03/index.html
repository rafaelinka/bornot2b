06 / The Brief

The Brief

Eleven reports on equipment, food, energy, distribution and payments. Here is what changed and what each announcement still leaves open.

November 12 · Spain and Finland

Algeciras goes back to Kalmar for 21 more carriers

TTI Algeciras ordered 21 hybrid straddle carriers from Kalmar, with delivery due to be completed in the fourth quarter of 2027. The terminal already operates 23 Kalmar carriers and uses the supplier’s maintenance services. A separate Kalmar Care agreement covers preventive and corrective maintenance for 40 terminal cranes.

Editorial view. For buyers, it is worth pricing a switch in suppliers with spares, training and downtime factored in. For suppliers, maintenance is part of earning the next order. The order was announced, but the price and actual fuel savings from the new fleet are undisclosed.

Kalmar

November 10 · India and Japan

An Indian shipyard orders six engines from a Japanese manufacturer

MITSUI E&S won its first order from an Indian shipyard. Swan Defence and Heavy Industries is building a series of six chemical tankers in Gujarat, and the Japanese manufacturer will supply dual-fuel engines under an Everllence licence. The licensor separately confirmed the technology selection on November 11. Industrial buyers can use this deal to ask who designs the component, who manufactures it and who integrates it into the finished product. The announcements did not disclose the engine price, warranty terms or delivery dates.

MITSUI E&S · Everllence

November 13 · Canada and the United States

A walnut processor sees a second use for its pasteurisation line

Agri-Neo said Hammons Products had installed its Neo-Pure pasteurisation technology for black walnuts at its Stockton, Missouri, facility. Hammons’ president says the line can also handle contract pasteurisation for other companies. Work from outside customers could help fill the line and improve returns on the equipment, provided peak-season capacity is available. The installation was reported by the supplier, whose announcement included comments from Hammons’ president. Processing capacity, service fees and the first third-party contracts were undisclosed.

November 12 · Switzerland, Costa Rica and Latin America

Santhera signs a 31-country distribution deal, with Brazil first

Santhera and SteinCares signed an exclusive distribution agreement for vamorolone across 31 countries in Latin America and the Caribbean. Santhera will receive a share of net sales. Brazil is the first market in the rollout plan, where SteinCares intends to pursue registration and explore an exceptional import route.

Editorial view. For manufacturers in regulated industries, the deal shows why distributors need country-specific milestones. The share of net sales, commercial shipment dates and realised sales are undisclosed.

Santhera · SteinCares

Deal Terms · Kobelco × CNH · Japan / United Kingdom · November 9

Kobelco plans to take control of CNH’s construction equipment business

CNH and Kobelco Construction Machinery announced a definitive agreement on November 9. Kobelco is due to acquire 70% of the construction equipment business through two entities in the US and UK, with an enterprise valuation of US$1 billion. CNH would retain 30% and says its agricultural dealers will continue to have access to construction machines supplied by the venture after closing.

Editorial view. For dealers, continuity comes down to who supplies spare parts, honours warranties and extends credit once control changes. The parties expect to close by the end of 2027, subject to approvals. Dealer prices, stock arrangements and warranty transition terms were not disclosed.

How They Sell · Flowers Foods · United States · November 12

Store-brand bread and foodservice are moving in different directions

For the 12 weeks ended October 10, Flowers Foods’ net sales fell 4.7% to $1.169 billion. Branded Retail net sales fell 5.1% to $772.1 million. Other net sales fell 4.0% to $397 million. Lower store-brand sales, partly from discontinued business, were partly offset by foodservice growth. The filing gives no separate revenue or margins for those two channels.

Editorial view. Track orders, cost to serve and margin by channel. A blended result can mask a loss of business in one channel and growth in another.

Flowers Foods · SEC filing

Procurement · FairPrice Group × Tuas Power · Singapore · November 10

A grocery group has lined up electricity for 2030–2040

FairPrice Group announced an 11-year agreement with Tuas Power to procure biomass-generated electricity from 2030 to 2040. The named source is the Tembusu facility. FairPrice plans to verify the renewable origin through certificates and to apply feedstock sourcing and traceability measures. The feedstock includes imported agricultural waste and local waste wood. The announcement gives no price, volume or share of the buyer’s consumption.

Editorial view. A long contract calls for a delivery schedule, arrangements for any shortfall and evidence the buyer can use to verify the electricity’s origin.

FairPrice Group

Routes to Market · Hon Chuan × Nihon Yamamura Glass · Taiwan / Japan · November 11

Beverage packaging gets a joint sales channel in Japan

Hon Chuan and Nihon Yamamura Glass announced an agreement to set up a sales joint venture in Japan. The plan pairs Hon Chuan’s packaging, contract filling and production network with Yamamura’s local quality and logistics services. The Japanese release dates the agreement to November 2 and the Taiwan release to November 11. Neither discloses ownership shares, the launch date or initial orders.

Editorial view. Before ordering, a buyer needs to know who handles a defective closure, issues the invoice and holds local stock. A shared sales channel leaves those questions open until the parties assign the work.

Payments · Payments Canada · Canada · November 9

The payment arrives. Can accounting match the invoice after hours?

Payments Canada launched the Real-Time Rail with eight active participants. The system exchanges, clears and settles payments in real time, around the clock, using ISO 20022 messaging. Paramount Commerce said eligible clients can access the rail through its service. Access, limits and fees depend on the provider’s offering. Estimated savings of up to C$14.5 billion over a decade are a C.D. Howe Institute projection.

Editorial view. Check whether both sides support the transfer, whether the invoice reference reaches the recipient’s accounting system and who deals with unmatched payments outside business hours.

Payments Canada · Paramount Commerce

Visa adds another way for eligible partners to settle

09 / Beyond the announcement

Beyond the announcement

Three developments we would look for after these announcements.

Did the warehouse make the handoff?

Compare the promised delivery date with the planned and actual carrier acceptance times. That shows whether the warehouse handed the parcel over in time for the run.

Did the first cargo get paid for?

For Lindian, watch for the actual shipment, confirmation of concentrate quality and payment under the letter of credit. January is the announced target.

Did an outside retailer get a quote?

The first comparable request to JD.com for warehousing or logistics should reveal what a retailer is offered on price, service scope and lead time.

10 / The Monday Move

The Monday Move

Take one promise in a live agreement, such as next-day delivery. Bring an actual order that missed the date to the next review.

  1. Commercial leadWrite down exactly what the customer was promised and when it applies.
  2. Operations teamCompare timestamps from order to receipt and find the first missed deadline.
  3. Both sidesAssign an owner for that step. Agree which number you will review in a week and where each side will get the data.
