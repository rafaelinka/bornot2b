Tool Watch

Scheduled checks for parcel invoices

Sifted added templates for parcel cost alerts. A small pilot can test whether the alerts point to recoverable overcharges.

SiftedAI · November 12 · United States · 1 min read

Hardware, documentation and production know-how involve different work. An editorial metaphor.

How to run a pilot

Sifted added a template library to SiftedAI for monitoring fuel surcharges, invoice accuracy and late deliveries. Scheduled checks themselves were announced in June. For a pilot, pick one surcharge in your current carrier contract, compare alerts against a sample of invoices and count how many discrepancies hold up under manual review. This gives the team a way to judge whether the alerts are worth the time they take to investigate. We have not tested the tool. The announcement did not report measured savings or alert accuracy.
