Peppol: Paid is not a bank statement

November's document update reveals the boundary between a technically valid invoice, completed commercial performance, and cash.

In This Analysis
  1. What changed
  2. Four facts that should remain separate
  3. A practical check before updating

Review status: validation not run. Peppol is a network and a set of document-exchange specifications, rather than an application we installed. We checked the primary release notes on November 27, 2026; we did not test a particular provider, ERP, or company's document flow.

An invoice can be technically correct and commercially disputed. It can enter processing while the goods are still being inspected. Document processing can also finish without a new transfer—for example, with a credit note. Finance needs to distinguish those events. If an interface combines them into one green checkmark, automation creates false confidence.

What changed

November's BIS 3.1.0 was published on November 23. In Billing, several identifier checks move from warning to fatal after a transition period. An error that previously accompanied a document as a warning can now stop validation. This warrants checking master data and rejection handling, rather than declaring all historical invoices invalid.

The other procurement-document notes clarify Invoice Response, including self-billing: when the buyer issues the document, the seller sends the response. They also explain Paid for credit notes: it signals that document processing is finished and no further action is expected, rather than serving as remittance advice. Do not automatically interpret that label as a new cash receipt.

The current primary notes for both Billing and the other BIS documents specify mandatory use from February 22, 2027. However, indexed versions of those same primary pages show February 24. Our November 27 check found public versions out of sync. Confirm the date for the specific profile with your provider before scheduling a switch. A specification release date is not a universal legal deadline for every company in every country.

Four facts that should remain separate

EventWhat it establishesWhat it does not prove
A document passes validationCompliance with the tested technical rulesAcceptance of goods or agreement with the amount
An invoice response arrivesA reported status for a particular documentA matching bank transaction
Paid on a credit noteCompletion in the specified Invoice Response senseA necessarily separate cash receipt
A bank entry existsA recorded movement of moneyCorrect allocation across every invoice

Our interpretation: finance needs separate evidence for the document, performance, and cash. Even a bank entry requires reconciliation to an obligation. One transfer can settle several invoices, while a credit note can reduce the amount due. These examples explain the risk of collapsing statuses; they do not describe your particular system.

A practical check before updating

A de-identified set can be small: a valid invoice, a copy with a deliberately invalid identifier, and a credit note. Record the rule version and profile. First establish that the error is caught before transmission. Then trace who receives the rejection and who corrects the authoritative customer or supplier record. Repairing XML while leaving the master dirty only postpones the next incident.

Costs and responsibilities need an owner too. Our proposed division gives the supplier ownership of master-data quality and the order relationship, the integration team ownership of mapping and error logs, and accounting ownership of ledger meaning. That is an operating recommendation, not a specification requirement. Leaving everything to the network provider can produce reliable transport while a commercial exception has no owner.

Next, examine the route from an external status into the ledger. What does the provider transmit, which internal field changes, and what action follows: closing a receivable, notifying sales, or stopping collection? For a credit note, specifically check that no fictitious cash receipt is created. Agree those actions with accounting; an external code's name is insufficient.

Finally, assign a person to exceptions. They need the original document, error description, validation version, and order relationship. Readiness means an explainable outcome for every document, including rejected ones, rather than merely a high percentage of successful transmissions. A standards update does not measure payment discipline. It can, however, reveal where the business has substituted a convenient interface label for evidence.
